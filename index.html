Ordering

How to order

Choose your design, your size and the quantity, then send us the details.

  1. Choose your design

    Browse the designs above. Note the code of the design you like.

  2. Pick your size

    Choose a size from the list, or ask for a custom size with your measurements.

  3. Send your order

    Call, message, or email us with the design code, size and quantity.

  4. Confirm and pay

    We confirm availability and the total. You can pay in cash, by EFTPOS or by bank transfer.

Payment, processing & delivery

How your order works

1. Shop and pay

Add designs to your cart and checkout. Pay the total into our BSP or Kina Bank account by mobile or internet banking, using your full name as the payment description. Then enter your receipt reference and the amount you paid.

2. We make your order

Customised orders usually take 1 to 2 weeks, depending on how many items you order.

3. Pickup or delivery

Delivery days are Fridays and Saturdays. At checkout we suggest a pickup date for your order.

Need it urgently? If you are travelling or need your order sooner, WhatsApp us on 76628003 with your order reference and details, so we can prioritise it.
